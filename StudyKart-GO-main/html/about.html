About StudyKart GO!

StudyKart GO! is an all-in-one productivity dashboard designed for students. It offers a clean, simple, and local-first solution to academic management.

Our mission is to provide a central hub where you can manage your courses, track your tasks, monitor your grades, and time your focus sessions without any clutter.

A local-first solution to academic management.
